Privacy Policy — All Browser Cleaner

Published by Verokod • Effective date: 22 October 2026

All Browser Cleaner is a Windows utility that deletes browsing data (history, cookies, cache, site storage, site settings and autofill data) from browsers installed on your own PC, at your request. This policy explains exactly what the app does — and does not do — with information.

1. We collect nothing

All Browser Cleaner does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting and no telemetry. We could not hand over your data if asked — we never receive it.

2. Cleaning happens entirely on your PC

Every cleaning action runs locally on your machine. Your browsing history, cookies and other browser data are read and deleted on your own disk only. None of it is uploaded anywhere, at any time.

3. The one download: sponsor banners

The app shows small sponsor banners inside its window. To keep those banners current, the app downloads a small configuration file and banner images from our public GitHub page (github.com/verokod/all-browser-cleaner) when it starts. This is a one-way download of images and display settings. It sends no information about you, your PC, your browsers or your usage. If you are offline, the app simply shows the last downloaded banner and works normally.

4. Links inside the app

If you click a sponsor banner or another link inside the app, your default browser opens the linked page. That website's own privacy policy then applies. We do not control third-party websites.

5. Children

All Browser Cleaner is a general-purpose PC utility and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new effective date.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy or the app: reach us via GitHub — github.com/verokod.
