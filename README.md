## What it cleans

- Browsing history & downloads
- Cookies
- Cache
- Site storage
- Site settings & permissions
- Autofill data

Bookmarks and extensions are **never** removed by normal cleaning. Saved passwords and extension storage are **off by default** and require an extra confirmation. Selected browsers are asked to close gently first — force is used only if a browser does not respond.

## Protect & Settings

- **Protect lock** — mark any browser as Protected in the Settings tab and it can never be closed or cleaned, even by accident.
- **Remember my selections** — your browser ticks are kept between runs.
- **Activity log, in your control** — every clean ends with a summary of what was cleaned and how much space was freed, per browser, per data type. Copy it, export it, clear it, or switch log saving off in Settings.

## Supported browsers

Detection is fully automatic. You can untick any browser to leave it completely untouched — an unticked browser is never closed and never modified.

All installed browsers are detected automatically — including Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, Mozilla Firefox, Opera, Vivaldi, DuckDuckGo, and more. If a browser is ever missed, it can be added manually and is remembered for future runs.

## Private by design

All cleaning happens on your own PC. All Browser Cleaner collects no data, has no accounts, and uploads nothing — your browsing data never leaves your machine. One honest exception to "offline": the small sponsor banners shown inside the app are downloaded from our public page on GitHub. Downloading a banner sends no information about you or your PC.
