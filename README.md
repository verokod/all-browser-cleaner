# All Browser Cleaner

**Every browser. One click. Completely clean.**

Clean history, cookies, cache and site data from every installed browser in one click. Private by design — cleaning runs entirely on your PC, and no data is ever collected or uploaded.

- **[Download All Browser Cleaner Setup.exe](https://github.com/verokod/all-browser-cleaner/releases/latest/download/All.Browser.Cleaner.Setup.exe)** (Official Windows Installer)

Free • Windows 10 & 11 • No sign-up

> **Install note:** All Browser Cleaner is new software, so Windows SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC" on first run. Click **More info** → **Run anyway** to continue.

## What it cleans

- Browsing history & downloads
- Cookies
- Cache
- Site storage
- Site settings & permissions
- Autofill data

Bookmarks and extensions are **never** removed. Saved passwords and extension storage are **off by default** and require an extra confirmation. After every run, a summary shows exactly what was cleaned and how much space was freed — per browser, per data type.

## Supported browsers

Detection is fully automatic. You can untick any browser to leave it completely untouched.

All installed browsers are detected automatically — including Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, Mozilla Firefox, Opera, Vivaldi, DuckDuckGo, and more. If a browser is ever missed, it can be added manually and is remembered for future runs.

## Private by design

All cleaning happens on your own PC. All Browser Cleaner collects no data, has no accounts, and uploads nothing — your browsing data never leaves your machine. One honest exception to "offline": the small sponsor banners shown inside the app are downloaded from our public page on GitHub. Downloading a banner sends no information about you or your PC.
