What it cleans

You choose the data types. You choose the browsers. Everything is unticked-safe by default where it matters.

Browsing History & Downloads

Visited pages and download history, cleared across every selected browser at once.

Cookies

Tracking and session cookies removed, so sites start fresh on your next visit.

Cache

Cached images and files that pile up over time — freed in seconds.

Site Storage

Local storage and offline site data left behind by websites.

Site Settings & Permissions

Per-site permissions and content settings reset, surgically — nothing else is touched.

Autofill Data

Saved form entries cleared on request.

Protect Lock

Mark any browser as Protected in Settings — its tick box locks, and it can never be closed or cleaned, even by accident. Microsoft Edge is protected by default.

Settings That Remember

A dedicated Settings tab keeps your choices between runs — protected browsers, your browser selections, and whether the activity log is saved.

Activity Log, In Your Control

Every clean ends with a clear summary. Copy the log, export it to a file, clear it — or switch log saving off entirely in Settings.

Careful by design

Selected browsers are asked to close gently first — force is used only if a browser does not respond. Browsers you leave unticked are never closed and never modified. A built-in Check for Updates button always points you to the latest release.

Protected by default

Bookmarks and extensions are never removed. Saved passwords and extension storage stay off by default and ask for an extra confirmation before they can be selected. A clear summary shows exactly what was cleaned, and how much space was freed — per browser, per data type.

Works with your browsers

Detection is fully automatic — installed browsers are found on their own, and you can untick any browser to leave it completely untouched. If a browser is ever missed, you can add it manually — it is remembered for every future run.

How it works

  1. Download and run All Browser Cleaner — your installed browsers appear automatically.
  2. Tick the browsers and data types you want to clean. Unticked browsers are never closed or modified.
  3. Click Clean. Selected browsers are closed gently, cleaned, and a summary shows what was removed and how much space was freed.
Install note (direct .exe download only): the .exe installer is new software, so Windows SmartScreen may show “Windows protected your PC” on first run. Click More info → Run anyway to continue. The Microsoft Store version does not show this warning.

Private by design

All cleaning happens on your own PC. All Browser Cleaner collects no data, has no accounts, and uploads nothing — your browsing data never leaves your machine. One honest exception to "offline": the small sponsor banners shown inside the app are downloaded from our public page on GitHub. Downloading a banner sends no information about you or your PC.
