About Verokod

Simple, privacy-first tools for Windows.

Verokod builds simple, privacy-first tools for Windows. The name says what we stand for: vero — true, genuine — and kod — code. Software that does exactly what it says, and nothing it doesn't.

All Browser Cleaner is our first product. It was built for a simple reason: most PCs have more than one browser installed, and cleaning them means opening each browser's settings one by one and digging through the same menus again and again. All Browser Cleaner finds every installed browser automatically and clears history, cookies, cache and site data from all of them together — in a single run, with a clear summary of what was removed.

What we believe

  • Private by design. Our software collects no data, has no accounts, and uploads nothing. Your information stays on your PC.
  • Your choices stay yours. Browsers you don't select are never closed or modified. Bookmarks and extensions are never removed. Sensitive options like saved passwords stay off by default and always ask first.
  • Honest about money. All Browser Cleaner is free. It shows small, clearly labelled sponsor banners inside the app — that is how it is funded — and anyone who finds it useful can support its development voluntarily on Ko-fi. Support never unlocks features and never removes the banners; it is simply a thank-you.
